## B. Ishaan: kids' Hindu philosophy for NRI families (10 episodes, 20 s, about $3.30 each)

### Character sheet (original, not Chhota Bheem)

- **Ishaan**, 7, a curious Indian-American boy. Round glasses, short curly black hair, teal kurta over jeans, red backpack. No topknot, no dhoti, no strongman gimmick, nothing that echoes Bheem. A gentle, thoughtful problem-solver.
- **Dadi** is the narrator only, an off-screen warm grandmother's voice (delivery `voiceover`). She calls him *beta* and explains one idea in a sentence or two. She is never shown, which keeps one character to keep consistent.
- **World:** a New Jersey apartment (kitchen, living room, balcony) and a nearby park. Diaspora-relatable, with Indian touches (diyas, dal, rangoli) but no deities depicted.
- **Episode shape:** (1) Ishaan voices a small kid problem, 6 s spoken. (2) Dadi gives the idea with one Sanskrit word, 8 s voiceover. (3) Ishaan applies it, 6 s spoken. The last line is the takeaway and loops back to the opening mood.
- **Needs your review:** philosophy accuracy and tone. Disclosure and made-for-kids = Yes on this channel. Separate channel recommended. Not wired up yet: a second channel config (character block, narrator voice, CTA) is needed before rendering.
