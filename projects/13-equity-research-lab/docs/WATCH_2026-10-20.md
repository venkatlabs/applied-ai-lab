# Watch: triggers, calendar, daily monitor, digest, refresh — 2026-10-20

Built in the order proposed in `THESIS_WATCH_LEARNINGS_2026-10-19.md`. No step spends AI credits unless you press a button.

1. **Triggers with status** (`lib/triggers.ts`, `components/StockTriggers.tsx`, `/api/triggers`): kill and upgrade triggers per stock, status armed / watch / tripped computed in code. "Start from this report" seeds kill triggers from the report's thesis killers and review checks, and an upgrade trigger at the hurdle price; it never invents a price threshold. Loosening a kill trigger (lower/raise threshold the easy way, extend or remove a date, reword, clear a tripped one, or remove it) needs a reason and is logged as a goalpost move with an alert. Tripped is sticky until you clear it. A tripped upgrade trigger only asks for a re-review.
2. **Calendar** (`lib/calendar.ts`, `/watch`): trigger test dates plus report review dates, grouped by Monday-start week, with overdue items.
3. **Daily monitor** (`/api/cron/monitor`, `lib/server/monitor-run.ts`, `lib/monitor.ts`): Vercel Cron at 21:30 UTC on weekdays. No LLM. Refreshes one price per followed stock (max 60 symbols, metered by `MONITOR_DAILY_CAP`, default 80), re-evaluates price triggers, alerts when a stock crosses into or out of its hurdle price, writes a job-health row. Never invents a price: a missing price or date is skipped.
4. **Weekly digest** (`/api/cron/digest`, `lib/digest.ts`): Sunday 14:00 UTC. Stored per account and shown on `/watch`: tripped and watched triggers, verdict changes, goalpost moves, next two weeks. No email or push is sent.
5. **Refresh when a dated test has passed** (`/watch`): lists stocks whose test date passed since their last report and offers one confirmed, budget-capped batch (max 5). Paid research never starts on its own.

## Setup (owner)
- Run `supabase/migrations/008_watch.sql` (after 006 and 007).
- Set `CRON_SECRET` (16+ random characters) in Vercel and redeploy. Without it both cron routes return 401 and `/watch` says no job has run. Vercel Cron sends it automatically.
- Optional: `MONITOR_DAILY_CAP`.

## Limits
- Price source is the existing market provider's end-of-day quote; triggers are daily, not intraday.
- Alerts live in the app only. No outbound notifications yet.
- Hurdle-crossing alerts depend on a saved five-case model; stocks without one only get trigger alerts.
